## Rationale
- Two stages. Type level first: every placeholder becomes a fold, an absorption into its parent, a survivor or a wall (T6, T7, T16-T21), so sources are typed with real types and never with fragments. Source level second: every pointer source is typed and its casts spelled as member accesses, region by region (T15, T22-T29), then the residue (T30). The mechanical bulk is tool work (3.40 T4 spelled 55,583 sites in 24 batches of 100 files with tool runs only); the judgment is a fragment's parent, a two-type source, a source with non-cast uses and every byte refusal.
- Order: lever_census's cache order (T4, before any batch compares its output); the source census (T14: measure first, the cast-view metric and the candidate parents, no source change); the series (T5, so every task-close snapshot records the fall); the fold engine and the bar in the standards (T6); the shape and linked folds (T7); the consolidation gate and DECL-PARAM (T8, before any parameter is retyped); the typing engine (T15); the pilot (T16, review); the absorption waves (T17-T21); the typing waves (T22-T29); the residue (T30); the milestone gate (T11), the docs (T12, rule T5), the cleanup (T13).
- T2 is marked done as-is: its tool, its ledger proposals and its report are this plan's inputs. The measurement it lacked (the pointer sources of locals and returns, candidate parents, cast views) is new tool work with its own done-when (T14), not a reopening. T3 (rename tooling), T9 and T10 (names) move to 3.46 (REPLAN decision 6); the type-token retype and member respell a fold needs are built into T6's engine on restruct's rung L, and 3.46's tooling reuses them.
- Task ids are kept where the subject stays (T4, T5, T6, T7, T8, T11, T12, T13, rewritten for the contract), so R178 (the Ghidra stop at T11) and R179 (DECL-PARAM in T8) still point at their tasks; new work takes T14-T30 in run order.
- Wave sizes from the numbers. Absorption: about 2,850 types after T7, about 120 in the pilot, the rest in five waves of about 550 by family, the largest family first (Unkstruct_800B5CB8, Unkstruct_80126B58 and their views); a coder run judges about 40-50 cases (3.43 T11: 113 functions in 3 runs), four judging coders a round, about 12 coder runs a wave. Typing: about 430k-495k cast sites; eight regional waves of 33k-85k sites and at most about 720 overlay files each (3.40 T4's 55k-site task; B1's 6,478 files are mostly small shared bodies), ov_SC03 (171k) split in two; shared bodies first, since one SHARED_FN body serves every overlay that includes it. - The pilot stops for the developer (review: yes) because it sets what a modder will read: the parent model for variant data and five functions before and after. Its review offers the wave plan, never a narrower finish.
- Effort: the developer's word (REPLAN decision 5) makes the judgment tasks `effort: high` with their coders on high: the twin review (T7), fragment tracing and source typing (T16-T29) and the residue (T30), 16 of the 25 open tasks, beyond the planning default of one in five. The tool tasks (T4, T5, T6, T8, T14, T15) are arbitrated by selftests and bytes and run at medium.
- Tools: extend, never add without §3's three lines in the task summary: struct_twins.py (T1 and T2's instrument) over type_census's sites and flows for the source census; restruct.py's rungs L, D, S, S2 and R for folds, retypes and spellings, since their byte oracle and ledger exist.
- Determinism (C0582): fixed input order and stable tie-breaks in every list a batch or a gate reads.
- The gate ratchets: tools-health must stay green while pending rows exist during the waves, so the consolidation check fails on a broken row or on any count above its committed ratchet, each batch lowers the ratchet in its own commit, and `--final` (T30, T11) fails on any pending row.
