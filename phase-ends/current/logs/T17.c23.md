- generalizable: a regex `FIELD\((\w*)\s*(\w+),` meant to skip an `M2C_` prefix backtracks into the argument (`arg0` -> `arg` + `0`); anchor the holder
  as `M2C_FIELD\(\s*(\w+)\s*,`. Cost c17 396 untraced holders.
- generalizable: after a per-source apply, the tree's typing is cast spelling (`((Parent *)v)->f`), not declarations; a re-classifier must read struct-view
  casts as typed seeds, and must accept union member spellings (`->unkCC.val =`) in its store scanners.
- generalizable: zero-arg cast-wrapped calls `((T (*)(void))g)()` and K&R calls into parameterless definitions carry $a0; add them only after the retail
  asm path check (no $a0 write, no call before `jal`): 472 of 8,563 failed it.
- generalizable: `guess_UpdateActorModelMatrix(h)` proves h's +0x20 is a model record; an access in [0x10C, 0x254) proves the player slot, whose +0x20
  is guess_g_PlayerModelObject.
- workflow: a typed seed on the source itself must be merged with the flow labels before deciding (var reuse hides a second pool).
