## What moved (no crack; the mechanisms, with evidence)

### func_80184828 - the commutative plus is swapped by combine
Best 1 (one instruction): ours `addu v0,v0,a1`, the target `addu v0,a1,v0`.
gcc-2.7.2 `combine.c`, `simplify_rtx` (its first action): "If this is a
commutative operation, put a constant last and a complex expression first" -
XEXP(x,0)=SYMBOL_REF is CONSTANT_P, XEXP(x,1)=REG, so the plus is SWAPPED.
The dumps show it: .rtl/.cse hold `(plus (symbol_ref D_80195AF6) (reg 118))`,
.combine holds `(plus (reg 118) (symbol_ref))`.
The target's tree must have held the pointer in a REG at combine time; only
the launder's `=r` output makes `p` a hard-reg pseudo. 55 tries over pointer
types, `&D_80195AF6[0]`, int casts, the struct-member spelling (`p[idx].f4`,
`((D_80195AF6_t *)((u8 *)p + off))->f4`), a temp-pointer address, the +4 in
`off`, `<< 3`, u32/u16 index, p used twice, decl order, the assignment before
the call - all 1.
Tag `combine-commutative-swap` (new; defined in the item's notes.md).

### func_8018483C - the constant address's copy dies at expand
Best 5. The target keeps `lui v0; addiu v0; move s0,v0`; every lever-free body
materialises the address straight into $s0. The .rtl dump of the lever-free
body (the `pb` temp plus `p = pb`) holds ONE set insn for the address
(`(set (reg/v:SI 81) (symbol_ref D_800B99DC))`): the copy is already gone at
expand, so cse never sees a second pseudo to copy. The target's copy needs a
short-lived pseudo for the constant plus the call-crossing one in $s0.
Cookbook §195-I: any SECOND set of the pseudo kills the fold - but the only
second sets available are identity statements (`p = p`), which the brief
rules out as launders. 34 tries (address used twice, pointer types,
`&D_800B99DC[0]`, a `pb` temp in a block, the pointer assigned before the
early return, loop shapes, the `for` init carrying the pointer, assignment
orders, `*p += c0`, the call args, the frame order).
Tag `cse-const-addr-copy` (new; defined in the item's notes.md).

### func_8017D81C - the constants are born at their first use
Best 11 (the base lever-free body is 20). With the launder, the asm READS
x/y/z at the assignment, so the pseudos are born there (insns 19-21, in
$v1/$a1/$a0). Without it cse forwards the constant into every use, the pseudo
is born at the FIRST USE, and the `li` lands just before the first store that
needs it. Diagnostic that settles it: moving the x-store to the front
(`var/w17_store_first.c`) puts `li v1,-1664` in insn 18 - the target's slot -
and drops the residual from 20 to 12; the target's store order forbids that
move, so the target's li's can only come from the launder's
birth-at-assignment. 104 tries (constant spellings, assignment orders, the
assignment before the call, declaration order, every early-store permutation,
do-while as for/while, the loop body's order, the store types, the pin on z
kept).
Tag `cse-const-holder` (new; defined in the item's notes.md).

## What the next shard should know

- The launder's power in all five rows is the same: it gives a pseudo a HARD
  REGISTER at the assignment point. What the target keeps (a reg first in a
  commutative plus, a constant materialised at its assignment, an arg setup
  at the top of an arm, a magic at the tail top) is exactly what a hard-reg
  pseudo buys; no C-level spelling found in 442 scored tries reproduces it
  without one.
- The counter-mechanism to look for is the SECOND SET of the pseudo
  (cookbook §195-I: `p += K`, `p = r;` kill the fold). Tested here and NOT
  reachable with an identity: `p = p;` is optimised away before cse, so it
  cannot kill the fold (both items stayed at their best). A REAL second set
  (a genuine second value) would be the only legal route - and no such set
  exists in these defs.
- The declaration-without-initializer form (`T p; ... p = CONST;`) does NOT
  stop the fold: it is cse, not the front end, that puts the constant into
  the address (the .rtl dump still shows the symbol before cse).
- gcc-2.7.2 sources are in `tools/reference/gcc-2.7.2/` (combine.c, cse.c,
  reload.c, loop.c; no expand.c). The swap that decides func_80184828 is
  `combine.c:simplify_rtx`'s first statement.

## Friction / gotchas

- The scratch compiler is C89: a declaration after a statement is a parse
  error, so every candidate's temporaries must sit at the top of their block
  (about a third of the generated variants died on that, and they count as
  no try).
- `tools/cc1_dumps_tu.sh` needs the TU's own directory on the include path
  (`-I<tu dir>`), otherwise the shared header is not found and the dump is
  empty (CPP-EMPTY); `_tools/dump_body.sh` in the shard does that and puts
  the dumps under the item dir. The three dumps were read and deleted again
  (the shard is 52 MB, under the 200 MB cap).
- `bin/dt.sh`/`bin/sc.sh` score one candidate per second, so a batch of 12
  variants is a minute: the breadth here was bought with batch generation
  (`_tools/gen.py` + `_tools/score.sh` in the shard), not with hand editing.
- No command was refused by the judge's permission rules in this shard.
