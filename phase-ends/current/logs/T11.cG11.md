## What cracked (2 MATCH)

- func_8018AA98 (ov_SC03_014) and func_80181CF4 (ov_SC03_097) — twins. The launder is the
  end-of-function out-only re-tie `__asm__ __volatile__("" : "=r"(pv));` whose comment says it
  "kills the sched1 birthing boost". It does: the `pv` load is a single-set def, sched1's S2
  birth boost sinks it to just before its consumer, and the 0xFF000000 constant is materialised
  early; the target has the load at its source slot and the constant just before its use.
  CRACK: split the mask into a COMPOUND assignment — `pv &= 0xFF000000;` — which is the only
  spelling that writes IN PLACE into pv's pinned $3 pseudo and therefore gives that reg a REAL
  2nd SET. The plain `pv = pv & 0xFF000000;` (a fresh temp, not in place) leaves the boost alive
  and scores 11 — the same as deleting the launder. Score 0, 177/177 ins, byte-identical.
  win.c is in each item dir; the pins ($4 ang, $3 pv) are kept — they are not the row kind.
  Generalisable: the lever census calls these "launder", but the mechanism is gcc 2.7's
  expand_assignment reusing the lhs location; a compound assignment is the C-level spelling of
  the re-tie.

## Walls (6), with the evidence each rests on

- func_801832A8, func_8018389C (ov_SC02_026, twins) — best lever-free 14. The launder is a
  barrier between the two magic-multiply chains (VOL /0xA0 and PAN /0x14 share the 0x66666667
  constant). Compound-splitting the VOL division (`vol = (0xA0 - av) * 0x7F; vol /= 0xA0;`)
  puts the VOL chain first and makes the instruction count exact (237 = 237), 30 → 14; the
  residual is the shared magic still sunk next to its mult instead of the tail top. Statement
  order (vol/pan swapped, pan-division first): 32/33. Pan as a compound division: 9071 (the
  function restructures). Tag sched1-birth-boost.
- func_8017DE84 (ov_SC02_039) — best lever-free 13. The v1 re-tie materialises a real
  `move v1,v0` (the target has it); plain C cse merges the copy, and without the re-tie the
  equality chain on v1 becomes a cse case table (3108 ins). The e re-tie keeps the unk20 load
  in the case1 arm (removing it: 6, the load sinks into the shared block). Tag cse-copy-merge.

  cse from building a case table (removing it alone: 6814 ins, a jump table). The v6 re-tie

- func_8017E780 (ov_SC03_092) — best lever-free 2, one instruction. The re-tie keeps the

- func_80180054 (ov_SC03_030) — best lever-free 48. Seven re-ties (xa, xb ×4, p) materialise

  `h`/`g` hold-space pair generates several variants in ONE sed run (without the initial `h`,
  `g` empties the pattern space and silently writes empty candidate files — that cost one
  bogus 9071 score).
