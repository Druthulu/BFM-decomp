## What the cc1 dumps say (the deciding fact for items 1, 2 and 5)

tools/alloc_table.py's priority is `floor(log2 refs) * refs * 10000 / live`; a tie is
broken by the allocno number (global.c:607, lower wins). A `register ... __asm__("$2")`
variable is a HARD-REG pseudo: its register is reserved for the WHOLE function, so the
load temps skip `$2` for the whole body. Lever-free, that reservation does not exist.

- func_8014FD54: the RTL carries `(set r73 v0)` (the call-result copy). Pinned, r73
  cannot take `$2` (r80 hard v1) and the copy survives as the ROM's `move v1,v0`.
  Lever-free r73 KEEPS the copy preference for `$2` and the local result r74
  CONFLICTS `$2` (r74 pri 11428 > r73's 10000, but the hard-reg preference wins), so
  the copy is coalesced away and the tests run on `$2`. Hoisting the mask into a
  variable splits the AND off the call copy (`andi v1,v0,0x8000`) and gives the local
  result `$2` — score 4, the best; the residual is exactly the missing `move v1,v0`.
- func_801536DC: the ROM's tail compares load into `$4`/`$3`; lever-free the load temps
  (local allocnos, pri 10000) own `$2`/`$3` in the first two comparisons and the value
  lands in `$4`, with the RESULT_DECL copy `move v0,v1` left over.
- func_8018655C: the counter and the call result of BOTH loops are four global
  LO_REGs (pri 40000 each) and take `$16..$19`; `r` (a normal pseudo, pri 15000) never
  reaches `$16`. The ROM's `r` in `$16` is a consequence of the two pins, not of the
  priorities. Taking the call result through a temp (the call-result pseudo stops being
  a LO_REG) took the score from 58 to 24 — the best; the residual is the cyclic
  rotation of the callee-saved bank.
