## Answer
Phases 3.38-3.41 unified struct definitions (fold head types, list tier-2 twins, lift types into canonical headers, gate by census), then mirrored the types into Ghidra. 3.43 named functions and globals only; its own record says struct types and fields were not named and the rename tools do not handle them. The 3.45 pass therefore starts from: one definition per type, twins apart unless use-site evidence binds them, `guess_` for any model-worded name with a basis row, and no field names without a cited source. Real-type count and field meanings are not recorded anywhere I found.
