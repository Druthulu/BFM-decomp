A new fold engine merges one placeholder type into its target, or absorbs a fragment into its parent. It retypes every use across the tree, renames field accesses by offset, deletes the old definition and keeps the side tables in step. Four pilot folds proved it on real types, with all 218 binaries byte-identical after each one.
