Decisions:
- binding: a callee's one prototype is its definition's. A different shipped view is written `RET_AS(T, fn, (params))` only at calls whose object needs it, and gets a ret_view_casts row with cites (exit writer plus caller read). Mismatches the cast cannot resolve stay in lying_exceptions. Documented in docs/ops/sig-evidence.md (Return views).
- a `void` definition whose callers read `$v0` is widened to the declarations' type and given a `$v0 at exit:` comment, unless that turns its many void declarations into new DECL-RET rows (func_8012EF70 took decl void + RET_AS instead).
