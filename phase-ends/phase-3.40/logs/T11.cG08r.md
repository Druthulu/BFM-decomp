  and copies at the return (`move v0,v1`). 40 ins both.

  tie by allocno and the temps (created first) take $2/$3. The RESULT_DECL is born at
  the return, so the temps may use $2 freely; the pin is what reserves $2.

  (p,q,i -> $16/$18/$19 vs the ROM's $17/$16/$18) and the load temps take $2/$3 where
  the ROM has $4/$3; 95 vs 91 ins. Partial-pin probes (not lever-free, so not results):

- func_8018655C (greg-alloc-order): lever-free the call result `t` takes $16 (more refs)
  and `r` takes $18; the ROM has t in $18 (pinned) and r in $16. 167 ins both. Partial
