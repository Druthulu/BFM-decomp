## The crack
All five defs are the SAME body (an RTP/positional-sound function) differing only in the function name, the
`V8_<fn>` struct typedef and two data symbols. Each carries one `launder`: the tied-operand asm
`__asm__("" : "=r"(vol) : "0"(vol));` on `vol`, whose job (per the def's own comment, lever 3) is to stop
sched1 hoisting the PAN magic-multiply (`(sx+0xA0)/0x14`) ahead of the VOL one (`((0xA0-av)*0x7F)/0xA0`).

Plain C fix: drop the asm and give `vol` a REAL 2nd set right before the call —
`vol = vol | 0x3000;` — while keeping the call arg spelled `(vol | (0x3000 | sx)) & 0xFFFF`.
The OR is value-preserving (the arg ORs 0x3000 anyway and masks 0xFFFF) and the redundant 0x3000 in the arg
is what makes fold birth `li a0,2715` BEFORE `ori a1,a1,0x3000`. 237 ins, score 0. The `$4`/`$5` pins stay
(they are not the row kind).

expression too; the redundant spelling is what fixes the birth order (it folds out of the value).
