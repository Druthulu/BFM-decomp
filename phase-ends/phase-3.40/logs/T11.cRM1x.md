## What cracked (items 2 and 3)

Two real-C levers, no pin, no `register`, no `__asm__`, no identity launder:

1. **flag2/flag3/flag4 as `s16`** (were s32). The `+ zr` addends were the only
   thing that kept the flag -> flag2 and flag2 -> flag4 copies alive; with s16 the
   sign-extend escapes cse's equivalence and combine folds it back into a plain
   move, so `move s4,s0` / `move s6,s4` / `move s0,s4` survive (flag2 $20, flag3
   $22, flag4 back into $16). Width sweep: 27 s16/s32/u16 combinations, all of
   them 24-25 (s16 for all three is the 24).
2. **The final call split into `ot`**: `ot = func_800D29F8(...); return ot;`
   (ot is dead after that call, so the code is the same). The call result is
   already in v0, so the copy costs no instruction; what it buys is **+2 refs on
   ot**, and greg's density `floor(log2 refs)*refs/live*1e4` then ranks ot
   (3*12/live) above w (3*15/100) — ot takes the first callee-saved $19/s1 and w
   the second $18/s2, the target's bank.

`w0` is used directly (the `w = w0` copy and the $18 pin are gone).
func_801889B8 is the twin of func_801886B8 (same shape, other names) and the same
two levers crack it unchanged.

## Wall (item 1, func_801815AC, pin $3 on `q`)

Best 4 after 72 scored tries (27 min): the same four rows every time — the two
`lw` of q and the two `sh` of the stores differ only in `a0` vs `v1`.
Tried: decl order of q/ptr/st/val, widths of q/val/st, `*(u16*)(q+2)` and
struct-typed and `u16 *p` pointer-reuse stores, the tail duplicated into the
arms, ptr inlined, the 0x9000 constant as a function-scope rematerializable
local (cookbook §368), and a 50-body decl x store sweep.

Mechanism, read off gcc 2.7.2's own dumps (`.greg`, `.lreg` of the lever-free
body):
- `q` is a GLOBAL pseudo (defined in both switch arms, used after the join):
  `.greg` order `73 74 76 83 72`; `73 conflicts: 73 76 29` — its conflict set
  holds neither $2, $3 nor $4, so the plain regno scan would give it $2 (wrong:
  the target needs the value in $2).
- The store's base address needs a HARD reg, so local-alloc emits a hard-reg
  copy of q into the first reg no local qty holds ($2 = the call result, $3 = the
  0x9000 const and the slt, so $4); that copy is q's **copy preference**
  (`73 preferences: 4`), and the preference overrides the scan, so q lands in $4.
- Control: with the call argument made a constant (no call-arg copy anywhere)
  the preference and the $4 landing are unchanged — the donor is the store's own
  address copy, not the call-arg copy.
- The target's $3 for q is reachable only with q a hard reg (the pin): with a
  pseudo, $3 is held by the 0x9000/slt qtys in the arm blocks and the
  block-granular conflict keeps the address copy out of $3.

Wall tag: `local-alloc-hardregcopy`.
