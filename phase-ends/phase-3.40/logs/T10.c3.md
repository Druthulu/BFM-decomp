## generalizable
- An identity copy cse can't merge but combine can: a sext/zext cast that is a no-op given nonzero_bits (S03, S07).
- A K&R definition with narrow parm types under an int prototype splits the param into incoming-reg and copy pseudos. This replaces caller-saved param pins and launders with no caller change (S19).
