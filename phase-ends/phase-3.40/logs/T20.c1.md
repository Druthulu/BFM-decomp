## Probe method
- probe.py: the def is extracted from the current TU (or header) and every `$4-$7` pin is rewritten to a plain decl (init kept). Both variants are scored with `delever_search.py --try <tu> <fn> <body> --body`; every orig scored 0/MATCH (the baseline holds on all 252). Both are dumped with `tools/cc1_dumps_tu.sh` on a spliced TU copy (a header is inlined into its first includer; TU-local `#include "x.h"` is rewritten to an absolute path, or the copy fails CPP-EMPTY).
- The first divergent pass is the start of the run of passes, ending at sched1 output, whose function bodies differ. The bodies are compared after canonical renaming: pseudos are numbered by first appearance; the pinned hard reg and its nopin pseudo both become `VAR`; expand temp pairs `(set P X);(set R P)` are collapsed; label numbers are dropped; `(set VAR VAR)` is dropped. If the sched1 output is equal, the allocator decides.
- Var->pseudo is paired by aligning the collapsed rtl/cse/combine sequences.
