## Walls

- func_8017D508 — `jump2-crossjump-arms`, best 2. Arm1 (cond1) and arm3 (cond3) are
  byte-identical 8-insn blocks (sh 32 / li 1536 / sh 34 / li -80 / sh 0,36 / sh 0,46
  / j join / sh 48). With the fence, the zero-width `asm_input ("")` insn splits the
  common suffix (base .jump2 dump: insn 177 sits between the zero stores and the k
  store). Without it, jump2's cross-jump merges them: reg renaming makes arm3's
  `(reg/v:HI 121)` store equal to arm1's `(reg:HI 106)` store, so even a
  const-vs-variable source spelling is no difference. The target keeps both arms and
  still cross-jumps elsewhere (arm2/arm4 share the tail at 14ec), so the ROM's source
  made the two arms differ in RTL in a way invisible in the asm. Statement-order
  changes do break the merge (114 ins) but always land one slot off, because the
  store that ends up last in source goes into the reorg delay slot instead of
  `sh v0,48`: floor 2 across 30 tries (all 5-store orderings of both arms, s16/s32/
  u16 k, init-at-decl, k at function scope, cast-pointer stores, cast on the value).
- func_80180610 — `sched1-load-delay-fill`, best 4 (43 tries). The fence keeps the
  two `-0x60` global stores out of the `lh v1,524(s0)` delay slot. Lever-free, sched1
