## The residual, exactly (3 rows, idx 10–12): `ori $s5,0x96` sits after `sw $s0 / li $s0,5` instead of directly under its `lui`
Mechanism (read from `dumps_s1top/s1top.i.sched`, T-116…T-143): the block-1 TAG LOAD (multi-set `t`, unboosted, pri 2) becomes ready at T-116
but every field store outranks it in `schedule_select` (`insn N has a greater potential hazard`, sched.c:2616/1318 — stores are fronted over the
load in the pri-2 group) so it LINGERS up the store stream; at T-138 the last other store (`sw 4(p) ← 0xE1000086`) issues, at T-139 the load is
`blocking … for 1 cycles` behind it and `sb 3(p)` (the only store that conflicts with the 4-byte tag word) cannot issue before the load, so the
ready list holds floaters only and `rank_for_schedule` (sched.c:2385, LUID tie-break) hands the empty cycle to the highest-LUID floater =
block 3's `ori 0x96`. It lands at post-sched1 position 12 and sched2 keeps that LUID. This is the §501-H "unboosted tag load lingers into the
store stream, its bubble takes the highest-LUID floater" mechanism, verbatim.

needs the boosted `li 0xFF000000` (mhi) to lose the boosted LUID tie against the index `sll` — i.e. mhi's set UID ABOVE the sll's (§501-H
sub-lever). Measured (sweep4 `after_oi_*`, V1/V2): that works for the schedule (no bubble) **but moves mhi's sched1 birth one slot later
(35 instead of 34) and flips the local-alloc contest back** — mhi vs ob (`la D_800AA60C`) for $t2/$t3 is decided by `qty_compare`

## Laws worth the cookbook (all read from the 2.7.2 source + dumps, byte-tested here)
* **A cse-foldable parameter copy is a re-association hazard: kill it with a dead `param = 0;`** — `fold_rtx` folds `(y1 + C2)` into
  `(a1copy + C1+C2)` whenever the copy's quantity is still valid; the dead set costs nothing (flow deletes it) and keeps the derived value a
  single-set, boosted pseudo. This is the spelling that gives an in-place `addiu $aN,$aN,K` chain where `x -= C` on the parameter would make the
  first subtraction a 2-set floater (Opus's idx-3 `addiu $a1`). 
* **A 2-death constant can never be callee-saved without a pin:** global `find_reg` pass 0 (global.c:904, `regs_used_so_far` seeded with
  call-used regs, :352) takes `$t8` before opening `$s5`. If the target has two lui/ori constants sharing one $sN, they were two single-death
  locals, and the second's `lui` sits right after the first's death because local-alloc's first-fit found the register free — not a pin.
* **Naming a big constant moves its boosted `li` by one slot** (variable set vs force_reg'd constant at the consumer): a zero-byte dial on a
  local-alloc density contest whose margin is <1% (here 2411 vs 2400).
* **The residual class "floater eaten by the lingering tag load's bubble" (§501-H) can be COUPLED to the mask contest** through the mhi birth
  slot; when it is, the remaining dial is an unboosted `p & m24` for the OT-chain gap, which no C spelling produces (combine.c:2309 restores
  `reg_n_sets` after merging a 2-set and; an asm launder is a delay-slot phantom).
