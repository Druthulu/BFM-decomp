## Generalizable lesson (for the cookbook, §474 addendum)
**A "proved" tree-level wall is only a wall for TREE-level constructs.** `expr.c`'s EXPAND_SUM address route has its own
algebra — the distributive law at `expr.c:5368`, constant association in `both_summands`, `plus_constant` folding into
symbols — that runs AFTER fold and can build rtx shapes (nested MULTs, symbol±const bases) that no folded tree carries.
When a -O0 residual is "the compiler merged my constants", spell the index as `(x + c) * k - c*k`: fold leaves
`MULT(PLUS(…))` alone, expand distributes it into the nested MULT, and the constant cancels against the symbol.
Companion law from d1: the `la; addu; lbu 0(r)` vs `lbu sym(r)` address shape is decided by whether the index rtx is a
MULT (non-legitimate → `force_operand` → `la/addu`) or a REG (legitimate `(plus sym reg)` → `$at` macro) — read it
before choosing `*` vs `<<`.
