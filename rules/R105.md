# R105 — No carry-audit section means audit blocked
id: R105 · group: - · status: active · tags: - · origin: PhaseEnd_Phase37 candidate, ratified 2026-09-29 · added: 2026-09-29

A PhaseEnd closed without a '### Carry audit — phase <N>' section is audited as "no tables"; the auditor returns blocked, not a hand-measured substitute.
