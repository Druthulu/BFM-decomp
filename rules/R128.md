# R128 — Phase records keep the narrative axis for the retrospective
id: R128 · group: - · status: active · tags: phase-end,logs,retrospective · origin: legacy memory, Drew 2026-08-31 · added: 2026-10-14

Phase records are a primary source for the future 0%-to-100% retrospective (a separate project after BFM; the first month
of transcripts is lost). Task logs and the PhaseEnd recap keep the narrative axis alongside the operational one: dead ends
with their reasoning, what each cost (tokens, wall-clock, a red binary), beliefs that proved false named as such, withdrawn
claims kept rather than quietly corrected, and dates. Narrative goes in the log and recap, never into a task summary or plan.
