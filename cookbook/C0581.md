# Regex scanners over our C miss two declaration shapes and hang on a third

- argcheck DECL (tools/argcheck.py:48) params class `[^;)]` stops at the first `)`: one-line externs with nested parens in the params (function-pointer params) are invisible to it. 3.44 T1 at 46ae5c20a6: 2,949 lines, 5 callees, 2,494 files. Multi-line extern declarations are invisible too: 28 lines, 13 callees, 15 files.
- K&R definition heads: the parameter declarations between `)` and `{` end in `;`. A scanner that treats `;` as "not a definition head" drops every K&R definition. 3.44 T1's I5 rescan first found 350 of 356 for this reason.
- A head regex with nested optional/repeated groups over whole files can backtrack exponentially and hang a full-tree scan. Match the head with a bounded or linear pattern (find `name(`, then balance the parens by hand) rather than `(\w+\s*)*`-style groups.
