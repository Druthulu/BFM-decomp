1. **The pad nops are LOAD-BEARING OUTPUT.** The stubs sit at a fixed slot stride (0x10/0x18 bytes);

2. **Spell `addiu $tN, $zero, K`, never `li`** — the retail opcode is addiu (0x24); `li` leaves the
