# C0532 — A rung-S sweep reshapes map types: hash names drift, frozen bands go stale, twins go SHARED
tags: structs,restruct,rung-S,census,band,twins · date: 2026-10-21 · phase/task: 3.40/T4 · origin: P3.40 T2/T4/T5 summaries

A rung-S sweep reshapes the struct-map type set; plan for it.

- Hash-named map types (`Unkstruct_<hash>`) are union-find cluster hashes over the raw sites the census sees; they change when the census sees new bodies or fewer raw sites. Key decisions on them for one pass only, and define under address names (`Unkstruct_<func/global addr>`) (3.40 T2).
- So a frozen band (a ranked list of types) does not stay complete after a sweep: new names appear over the remaining raw sites. Any "TNC 0" done-when needs a post-sweep decision pass over unranked types (3.40 T4).
- After rung S draws band twins into copies of one routine, those twins become SHARED dup classes in `--check-structs`; fold per shared-object component (3.40 T5; fold mechanics C0512).
